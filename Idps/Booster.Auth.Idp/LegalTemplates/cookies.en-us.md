This policy explains the cookies used on the sign-in and account pages provided by {{organization}}. Cookies are small text files stored in your browser. General information on how your personal data is processed is on the [KVKK Privacy Notice](/kvkk) page.

## In short

- By default, these pages use **only strictly necessary cookies**. Without them you cannot sign in.
- Your consent is not requested for strictly necessary cookies, but we inform you about them on this page.
- All cookies are first-party: they are set and read only by this service's domain.
- No advertising or tracking cookies are used.

All of these cookies are sent only over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. The session, language and anti-forgery cookies cannot be read by page scripts.

"KVKK" refers to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698.

## Optional cookies

The cookie preferences screen has two optional categories:

- **Functional cookies:** make things easier by remembering preferences such as the sign-in method you last used.
- **Analytics cookies:** help measure, in aggregate, how the pages are used.

These categories are **off by default** and are used only if you allow them. No cookie in these categories is set without your consent. In the default installation, no cookies are used in these categories; if {{organization}} starts using a cookie in these categories, it will be listed on this page.

## External sign-in providers

If you choose to sign in with an external provider such as Google, Microsoft or Apple, you are redirected to that provider's page. The provider may set its own cookies on its own domain. Those cookies are subject to that provider's cookie policy; they are not set or read by these pages.

## Changing your choices

You can change your cookie choices at any time on the [Cookie Preferences](/cookies/preferences) page. Withdrawing a permission you gave earlier is just as easy.

## Managing cookies in your browser

You can view, delete or block cookies in your browser settings:

- [Google Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Mozilla Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/kb/clear-cookies-and-site-data-firefox)
- [Microsoft Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09)
- [Apple Safari](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies-sfri11471/mac)

If you block strictly necessary cookies, you will not be able to sign in or the forms will not work. If you delete them, you will be signed out and your language and cookie choices will be reset.
