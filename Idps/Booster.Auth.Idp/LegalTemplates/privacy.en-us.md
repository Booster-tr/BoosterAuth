## What this service is

These pages are the identity service that lets you sign in to {{organization}}'s applications. You sign in here once, and connected applications recognize you through this service. The data controller is {{organization}}.

## What information we use

- Information that identifies your account: name, username, e-mail address, phone number.
- What is needed to sign in: a one-way hash of your password, and one-time codes sent by e-mail or SMS.
- What is needed for security: the IP address a session was opened from, browser and device information, sign-in times, failed sign-in attempts.
- If you sign in with an external provider (Google, Microsoft, Apple, LinkedIn, Facebook, GitHub, X, or an organizational provider) or with an organizational directory, the identity information that provider sends us.
- Roles and permissions assigned to you, and which of your details you allowed to be shared with which application.

The detailed list of what is processed and on which legal ground is on the [KVKK Privacy Notice](/kvkk) page.

- Verifying your identity and keeping you signed in to connected applications.
- Creating and updating your account and helping you reset your password.
- Protecting your account against unauthorized access and abuse.
- Meeting our legal obligations.

## How we protect your information

- Your password is never stored in plain text; it is stored with a strong one-way hashing algorithm (Argon2).
- Reuse of previous passwords can be blocked.
- The account is temporarily locked after repeated failed sign-in attempts.
- If {{organization}} has enabled it, a second verification step by e-mail or SMS is required at sign-in.
- Session and refresh tokens are stored in the database only as hashes.
- You can see and end your active sessions on your account page. When you change your password, your other sessions are ended; when you reset it, all sessions are ended.
- All connections are encrypted (HTTPS); session cookies are sent only over encrypted connections and cannot be read by page scripts.
- Forms are protected against cross-site request forgery (CSRF).
